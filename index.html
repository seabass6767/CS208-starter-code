Meridian, Idaho

Location

Meridian, Idaho is a growing city located west of Boise and about 25 minutes from Boise State University. It has a welcoming feeling, a nice downtown area, and easy access to shopping, restaurants, and outdoor activities.

About

Weather

Meridian has all four seasons, with hot summers and cold winters. Spring and fall are usually pretty chill, making it a nice place for outdoor activities throughout the year.

Population

The population of Meridian, Idaho is just over 149,000 residents. Fun fact, it is the second largest city in the state of Idaho.

Sports

All kinds of sports are pretty popular, especially the outdoor and competitive ones that are played in high school like Friday night football.
